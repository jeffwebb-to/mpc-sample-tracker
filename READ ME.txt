MPCTRACKER

Copy the "autorun" and "tracker" folders to the root of an SD card and insert it.
Save your MPC project first. The tracker starts a few seconds after the sampler boots.

Hold SHIFT and press STOP to quit back to the sampler. Your song is saved to
tracker/songs/AUTOSAVE.mst when you quit and loaded again next time.
Take the card out and power cycle for a normal boot.

PLAY / STOP        play, stop
SEQ RECORD         switch the pads between playing and writing
SCREEN 1 / 2 / 3   pattern, samples, song   (with SHIFT: save, load, new)
DATA               move; SHIFT + DATA changes the value under the cursor
PLUS / MINUS       next / previous column
PAD BANK           sample bank A to D
16 LEVELS          pads play one sample as a keyboard
NOTE REPEAT        pads are the 16 rows on screen
MUTE + pad 1-8     mute a track
CHOP               undo (SHIFT + CHOP is redo)
ERASE              clear
MAIN VOLUME        master volume

Put WAV files in tracker/samples, or browse to any folder on the card.
.mod files in tracker/songs can be loaded with SHIFT + SCREEN 2.

This is an unofficial modification. It runs from RAM and only writes inside the
"tracker" folder on the card. Use it at your own risk and back up your card first.
